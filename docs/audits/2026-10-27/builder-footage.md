# Limestone Ridge Homes (builder): footage audit (independent reviewer, 2026-10-27)

Verdict: fails. Evidence: scratchpad/audit/builder/ (may not survive the session). Shot 6 uses source 40-120.

## Blockers
- B1 Shot 5 interior (site 175-190, src 45-85): staircase dissolves into a wall; double-exposed lamps and sofa; console smears; fire goes out in one step (187->188); the double-height room with chandeliers and gallery becomes a single-height flat-ceiling room (191-200); camera sways. Fix: trim to src 0-45, or re-render a straight push-in with both ends pinned to one room still; kitchen as its own locked shot after a hard cut.
- B2 Shot 2 slab (054-080): truck materialises (no drive-in); forms pop in after the truck; slab straight onto clay (no vapour barrier, rebar or PT cables despite the caption); concrete appears ~25 m from the chute and fills toward it; crew teleports (~2 m per frame, src 54-62); phone never shows the truck. Fix: replace with no people or vehicles (locked time-lapse of stakes -> forms -> slab, or a push-in on the finished slab).
- B3 Shot 6 keys (201-240): family glides at ~5-13 m/s; trousers change colour (src 44->45); the father turns into the builder in a different jacket with a logo (src 100-102) and smiles at camera (231-240); the pivot door warps and changes wood. Fix: keep the house lighting up with the path empty; any handover as a faceless key close-up after a hard cut.

## Noticeable
- N1 House design drifts between framing, dry-in and finish (gable becomes hip roof, 2 -> 3 windows, small -> wide window, wrap -> board-and-batten, entry glass 2 -> 3 columns, roof colour); the blueprint overlay highlights it at 120-140. Fix: re-render shots 3-4 pinned to the finished master, or fade the overlay out by frame 110.
- N2 Shot 3 framing: a worker melts (088-089); two workers grow from a blob (118-120); the truck lingers then dissolves (100-102); the tree line thins (099-104); one day's light vs days 46->111. Fix: no people or trucks, from an empty-slab still; trim the end at src 112.
- N3 Shot 4 dry-in: one-frame car smears (123, 142, 143, 151-152); a peach blob at 128; lumber becomes a stone pallet (152-153); a worker dissolves (127-129) and another stands frozen; wrong build order (stone onto bare studs before roof, windows and wrap); scaffolding pops in at 144. Fix: re-render with no people or traffic and the correct order, or two pinned clips.
- N4 Phone crops: shot 1 oak cut to a sliver (hold ~0.40); shot 2 truck never in frame; shot 6 family cut in half; iPad portrait gets the soft upscaled phone set.
- N5 Window views inside (pool, open fields) don't match the suburban lot outside.

## Nitpicks
Oak canopy colour shifts (reads as seasons); sun direction inconsistent (shots 3, 4, 6); shot 1 jolt into the fog at 020->021 and fog brightness pops; construction pace vs counter; small faces (201, 231-240); site-level stalls and surges; study book spines pseudo-text; great-room furniture differs from shot 5; og.jpg and index use the same three-person group.

## Holds up
Sub-pixel locked camera across shots 2, 3, 4, 6; the shot 1 drone; walls rising as time-lapse pops; the finished house lighting up at dusk; all six room photos.

## Plan
Remove every person and vehicle from shots 2, 3, 4, 6 (a locked time-lapse of the house alone plus the dusk lights); trim or re-render shot 5; pin shot 4 to the finished master or fade the overlay early; an optional faceless key close-up; per-subject phone crops and a sharper iPad set.
