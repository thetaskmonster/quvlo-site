## Lesson
nano_banana_pro falls back to the older Escalade front even with a head-on reference photo. What works is a gpt_image_2_5 two-image edit ("redraw the front of image 1 to match image 2"), then upscaling.
Balance after: 629.75 (spent 97.0 of 100)
