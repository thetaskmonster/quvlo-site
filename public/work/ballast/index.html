Body recomposition

Same weight. Different body.

A bathroom scale adds everything together: muscle, fat, water, last night's dinner. Lift and eat well, and muscle goes on while fat comes off. The number barely moves, and that is exactly where most people quit.

At Ballast you see the parts separately. Lean mass, fat and where each one sits, scanned and read with your coach, so you know it's working before the mirror does.

How it works

Scan. Coach. Scan again.

  1. 01

    First scan

    A few quiet minutes on the scanner, then a sit-down with your coach to read it together. You leave knowing your lean mass, your fat and where each one sits.

  2. 02

    Train the plan

    Your coach writes three or four sessions a week and runs them with you on the floor, plus simple targets for eating around them. No crash diets, no punishment cardio.

  3. 03

    Rescan and adjust

    A quick scan every Monday shows the trend. Every fourth week your coach rebuilds the plan around what the scans actually say.
