Water on hot stone. That is most of the idea.

One round, about half an hour.

Contrast is the whole practice: let the heat open you up, let the cold close you down, then sit still long enough to notice.

  1. 1
    Heat

    Fifteen minutes in the cedar room at 190°F, or the infrared suite at 140°F if you like it gentler.

  2. 2
    Cold

    Two to three minutes in the plunge at 39°F. Long breath out as you go under.

  3. 3
    Rest

    Ten minutes in the lounge, robe on, phone away. Most people go around twice in an hour-long session.
