    Sheet G-4 · Notes

    Questions.

    Do I need my own lot?

    No. We can help you find one, or build on a lot you own. We assess lots before you buy, so you know about the soil, the slope and the utilities first.

    Will the price change during the build?

    Once plans and selections are final, the contract price is fixed. Changes you ask for later are priced before they are made.

    How long does a build take?

    Most homes take eight to eleven months from slab to keys. The estimate above gives a schedule for your size.

    Can I bring my own plans?

    Yes. We review them with our engineer, suggest changes that save money and price them line by line.
