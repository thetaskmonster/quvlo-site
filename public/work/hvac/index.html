    Asked in July.

    Is there a trip fee?

    The $99 repair visit is the trip and the diagnosis. If you go ahead with the repair, the $99 comes off the repair price. Tune-ups are one flat price with nothing added.

    Repair or replace?

    If a system is over about 12 years old and the repair is more than a third of the price of a new one, we will tell you, and show the math. A dead compressor on a 15-year-old unit is usually that case.

    How fast does the house cool down?

    Cold air comes out of the vents as soon as the system runs. A house that has baked all day usually drops one to three degrees an hour, so we set the thermostat, leave it running and it reaches your set point overnight.

    Do you work evenings and weekends?

    Yes, seven days. Same-day visits cost nothing extra. Evening and weekend repair visits cost more, and the price shows as soon as you pick the time.

    Do you do heating?

    Yes. Heat pumps heat as well as cool, and we service furnaces too. Book a heating check before the first norther.
