Black paint isn't dull. It's scratched.

Automatic washes and dry towels leave thousands of fine circular scratches in the clear coat. Each one catches the light, and together they turn deep black into hazy gray.

Correction levels the clear coat by a few microns until reflections go sharp again. Then we seal it, so the finish you paid for is the finish you keep.

Paint correction

Machine polishing in stages to remove swirls, haze and light scratches. Most cars need one or two stages. The inspection tells us which.

Usually one to three days in the studio

Ceramic coating

A hard, glassy layer bonded to the clear coat after correction. Water beads and rolls off, road film lets go with a rinse, and the gloss holds between washes.

Applied the day correction finishes

Paint protection film

Clear urethane film on the panels that take the hits: bumper, hood, mirrors and rockers. Stone chips land on the film instead of the paint.

Front end or full body

Interior restoration

Leather, Alcantara and carpet cleaned and conditioned, with steam for the places a vacuum can't reach.

Usually one day
