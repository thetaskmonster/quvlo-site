How coaching works.

One coach, one client, and a rhythm simple enough to keep when the week gets loud.

  1. 1

    An intro call

    Twenty minutes by video. Tell Noor what's going on. You'll get an honest answer about whether coaching is the right help right now.

  2. 2

    Sessions

    Fifty minutes, every week or every other week. By video, or on a walk if you're nearby. You bring what matters this week; the plan keeps the thread.

  3. 3

    The week in between

    A check-in on Sunday, a plan on Monday, one short follow-up midweek. That's where most of the change actually happens.

Between sessions, nothing gets lost.

Your portal holds everything you've written and everything you've agreed. It's private to you and Noor.

  • Sunday

    A check-in in your own hand

    Write on paper and snap a photo, or type it in. Either way it stays in your words.

  • Monday

    Your plan for the week

    Noor reads every check-in and writes back four or five things for the week, each one tied to a line you wrote.

  • Wednesday

    One follow-up

    A short note about the thing most likely to slip. Reply in a word, or don't. It still counts.

  • Before you meet

    No starting from zero

    Before each session, your last plan, how it went and what you wrote sit together on one page.
