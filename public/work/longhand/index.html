How coaching works.

One coach, one client, and a rhythm simple enough to keep when the week gets loud.

  1. 1

    An intro call

    Twenty minutes by video. Tell Noor what's going on. You'll get an honest answer about whether coaching is the right help right now. Noor is an ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC).

  2. 2

    Sessions

    Fifty minutes, every week or every other week. By video, or on a walk if you're nearby and don't mind being out in public. You bring what matters this week; the plan keeps the thread. Coaching runs in three-month blocks: $750 a month for weekly sessions, or $450 a month for every other week, both with weekly check-ins and follow-ups. We start with a short written agreement covering how we work, privacy and fees. Either of us can end it at any time.

  3. 3

    The week in between

    A check-in on Sunday, a plan on Monday, one short follow-up midweek. That's where most of the change actually happens.

Coaching looks forward: goals, habits, decisions. It isn't therapy, medical advice or a crisis service. If what you're dealing with needs a therapist or a doctor, Noor will say so on the intro call and help you find one.

Between sessions, nothing gets lost.

Your portal holds everything you've written and everything you've agreed. It's private to you and Noor. Noor shares nothing without your written OK, except where the law requires it or someone's safety is at risk. That's written into our agreement.

  • Sunday

    A check-in in your own hand

    Write on paper and snap a photo, or type it in. Either way it stays in your words.

  • Monday

    Your plan for the week

    Noor reads every check-in and reflects back the four or five things you said you'd do, each one tied to a line you wrote.

  • Wednesday

    One follow-up

    A short note about the thing most likely to slip. Reply in a word, or don't. It still counts.

  • Before you meet

    No starting from zero

    Before each session, your last plan, how it went and what you wrote sit together on one page.
