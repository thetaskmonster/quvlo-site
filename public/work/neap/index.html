Why Neap

Twice a month the sun and moon pull at right angles, and the sea hardly moves. That's the pace we keep.

Therapy at Neap is unhurried and steady. The same therapist, the same hour each week, fifty minutes at a time. You don't need to arrive with the right words, or know exactly what's wrong. Most people don't.

We start by listening, and we let the work go at the speed you can actually live with.

First session

What happens after you ask.

No phone tag, no long forms, and no pressure to commit before you've met anyone.

  1. A short reply

    Someone from the practice emails you to offer a few times that match what you picked. You choose one, or ask for others.

  2. Fifty minutes

    In the room or by video. You talk about whatever you choose to bring. Your therapist asks a few questions and explains how they like to work.

  3. Decide together

    At the end you both say whether it feels like a fit. If it does, you keep the same hour each week. If it doesn't, we'll suggest someone else.

Privacy

What intake asks, and what it doesn't.

A first request should take a minute and give away very little. The rest belongs in the room, with your therapist, when you're ready to say it.

We ask

  • What you'd like to be called. A first name or a nickname is fine.
  • One way to reach you: an email address.
  • Your three answers above, only if you choose to bring them.
  • Whether you'd rather meet in person or by video.
  • The time of week you could keep.

We don't ask

  • Your diagnosis, history or medication.
  • Why you're coming, in any detail.
  • Your full legal name, birthday or home address.
  • Insurance or payment details.
  • Your phone number or anyone else's.
