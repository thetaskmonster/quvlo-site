Questions

Before you book.

How does the deposit work?

We place a refundable hold on a credit card when you book. It is not a charge. We release it within three business days of the car coming back, minus any extra miles, fuel or damage, which we show you with photos first.

How old do I need to be?

25 or older, with a full driving licence held for at least two years and a credit card in your name.

How many miles are included?

150 miles a day, added up over the whole booking, so a quiet day covers a long one. Extra miles are charged per mile at the rate shown in your quote.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere within 30 miles of our garage: homes, offices, hotels and the private aviation terminals. Further out is possible on request.

What if my flight is late?

Add your flight number when you reserve and we track it. The driver waits for you at no charge, however late you land.
