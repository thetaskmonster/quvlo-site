Questions.

How does the deposit work?

A refundable hold on your credit card, not a charge. Released within three business days of return, minus any extra miles or fuel, shown to you with photos first.

How old do I need to be?

25 or older, with a full licence held for two years and a credit card in your name.

How many miles are included?

150 a day, pooled across the booking. Extra miles are charged at the rate in your quote.

Where do you deliver?

Within 30 miles of our garage: homes, offices, hotels and private aviation terminals. Further on request.

What if my flight is late?

Give us your flight number and we track it. The driver waits, at no charge.
