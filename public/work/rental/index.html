Questions

Before you book.

Is there a deposit?

A $500 hold goes on your card at delivery and drops off within a few days of return. The Escalade hold is $1,000. We never take it from a debit card.

How old do I need to be?

25 and up for every car. Drivers 21 to 24 can book the Model Y or the BMW i5 with a $25 a day young driver fee. The Escalade is 25 and up only.

How do tolls work?

Every car has a toll tag and every toll is in the daily price. You will not get a bill in the mail and there is no daily toll service fee.

Where do you deliver?

DFW and Love Field arrivals, plus hotels and homes in Dallas, Fort Worth, Arlington, Irving, Frisco and Plano. Farther out, ask us and we will quote it.

What if my flight is late?

We track the flight number you give us, so your driver adjusts on their own. Late night and early morning arrivals are covered.
