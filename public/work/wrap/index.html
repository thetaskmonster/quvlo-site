    Before you book.

    Does a wrap damage paint?

    On factory paint, a quality film removes cleanly when the time comes. Repainted panels are checked first.

    How long does it last?

    Typically several years with proper care. We go over washing and care at pickup.

    Is the tint legal?

    In Texas, front side windows must let in at least 25% of light. The slider above shows when a shade is rear glass only.
