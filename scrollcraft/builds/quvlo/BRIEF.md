  (dentists, med spas, law firms) and micro-agencies. Zero clients yet; the site is the portfolio.
- **What the visitor must believe by the end:** "This studio can see what my site is missing that I
  can't — and can build one that both people and AI can actually use."
- **What they do next:** one action, one label — **"Start here."**

## The eight interview answers

1. **★ Vibe (3–5 words) + references:** *precise, nocturnal, engineered, quietly luxurious.*
   References (not websites): a **planetarium orrery**, a **Braun / Dieter Rams instrument**, an
   **architectural section drawing**. (Deliberately avoids the sci-fi/hologram look.)
2. **Scroll journey (their sequence):** a single unbroken descent —
   Surface → Descent → The Lattice → Two Readers → **The Agent's Eye** → Return / Start here.
3. **★ Energy curve:** calm and assured at the surface → motion builds on the descent → controlled
   through the structure → tension as the two readers travel → **peak at the Agent's Eye** → settle
   and resolve into the CTA.
4. **Feeling curve (emotion → what causes it), one line per leg:**
   1. *Surface* — assured calm → a perfect instrument at rest.
   2. *Descent* — curiosity → the ground opens and we fall inward.
   3. *The Lattice* — respect / control → hidden order revealed, everything in its place.
   4. *Two Readers* — quiet tension → two travellers on one path; one might not make it.
   5. *The Agent's Eye* **[PEAK]** — recognition, a small jolt → the surface becomes what the machine
      reads, and most pages simply vanish here.
   6. *Return* — resolve, invitation → back to the surface, now understood; one clear action.
   - **Authored silence:** a held beat at the bottom of *Two Readers*, before the peak. Deliberate
     stillness so the reveal lands — mark it so verification does not read it as dead scroll.
5. **Signature move (the seed):** **The Agent's Eye.** At the peak, the rendered surface strips to
   the page's *real* accessible reading — the semantic/AX structure of this very page — shown beside
   the human view, then the human view falls away. Unrepeatable because it *is* the product: it
   reflects the actual document, not a picture of one.
6. **★ How far from premium-minimal:** **bold / maximalist** — but engineered-bold: dramatic motion
   and depth, instrument-dense, *restrained in taste.* No gaudy gradients, no neon, no AI-purple.
7. **Structure:** **one unbroken world** (worldflight). Kyle's call.
8. **Assets:**

## Art direction (the anti-slop guardrail)
Champagne light on near-black. Matte, physical, engineered — a lit scale model / orrery / section
drawing. **Banned** (both ScrollCraft's refuse list and Quvlo's own rules): AI-purple gradients,
glowing neon wireframe, hologram sci-fi, gradient text, em dashes in UI, invented stats, the
cream-and-brass artisan palette.

## Non-negotiable gate — accessibility (this IS the pitch)
A fixed-video worldflight is the hardest case, so the fallback is a first-class deliverable:
- **Reduced-motion → a static, fully readable, semantic document** (no video; copy as normal flow).
- **All overlay copy is real semantic HTML** (headings, landmarks, named controls) — agent- and
  screen-reader-readable. The Agent's-Eye signature is itself an accessibility feature.
- **Keyboard:** the CTA and any control reachable and visibly focused. Text ≥ 4.5:1 on the brightest
  frame under it. Must match the live site's standard, verified with the session's harness.
