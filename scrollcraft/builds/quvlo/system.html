Quvlo · design system

Two readers,
one surface.

Every page is two things at once: a surface a person sees, and a structure a machine reads. This system encodes that split in four pillars — warm is the human, cool is the machine — so the design doesn’t just describe Quvlo’s thesis, it performs it. Built for people and agents: AX-native.
