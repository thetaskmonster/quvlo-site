Deposit received

Your slot is booked.

Stripe has your deposit and a receipt is on its way to the email you gave. Nothing else is charged until the build is finished and you have seen it.

What happens next

Within one business day we email you to introduce ourselves and ask for what we need: your logo if you have one, your services and prices, and access to wherever the domain lives. There is no portal and no account to create.

Then the design direction. You see the concept before we build anything. If it is not right we refine it. If it is still not right, you walk away and owe nothing beyond the deposit.

Then the build. When it is finished you review it on a private link. The remaining 50% is invoiced at that point, and the site goes live once it is paid.

Anything to change?

Reply to the receipt, or email hello@quvlo.co. If you have paid by mistake, tell us before design work starts and we will refund it in full.
